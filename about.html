Our Story

From Startup to Industry Leader

Founded in 2019 by a group of former NSA and Cisco security engineers, Stackly began with a simple mission: make enterprise-grade cybersecurity accessible to every organization.

What started as a small team of five in a San Francisco garage has grown into a global company of 400+ security professionals serving over 2,800 clients across 40 countries.

Our AI-driven platform processes over 10 billion security events daily, identifying and neutralizing threats before they can cause harm. We believe that security should be intelligent, automated, and always on.
